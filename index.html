TechHub is a competition organized by IGENERGY scientific club, bringing together students to tackle real-world problems through creativity, teamwork, and innovation.

Participants will work in teams to develop innovative solutions to a given real-world problem within a limited time of 4 hours. The challenge encourages critical thinking, collaboration, and practical problem-solving skills.

At the end of the competition, teams will present their solutions to the jury, which will evaluate their ideas based on creativity, feasibility, innovation, and effectiveness.

REGISTRATION

The team must have at least 3 members and no more than 4 members.
