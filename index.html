TechHub is a university hackathon organized by IGENERGY Scientific Club. It brings together students from universities and higher education institutions across Oran to tackle real-world technological challenges.

Working in teams, participants analyze a problem, come up with an innovative solution, and build a prototype or proof of concept within 15 hours.

The event encourages creativity, interdisciplinary collaboration, and knowledge sharing, with potential guidance from mentors and project evaluation by a jury.
